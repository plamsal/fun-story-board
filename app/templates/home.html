How it works

  1. Answer a few small questions with a word or a short sentence: what you hope for, what weighs on you, who or what gets you through. Add a line for your friends if you like.
  2. Read today's story right away. You are in it, and so are your words. You also get your lucky number for the day.
  3. The story grows through the day as more friends add theirs, and shows which friends matched.
  4. Tomorrow morning, a new story begins.
